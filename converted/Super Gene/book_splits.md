## Book 1: First God's Sanctuary
- Chapters 1-424
- Major plot points:
    - Han Sen enters First God's Sanctuary as an under-equipped, mocked young hunter, then accidentally kills a sacred-blood black beetle and gains both his first sacred-blood beast soul and the mysterious black crystal.
    - The black crystal becomes his secret advantage, letting him mature low-level creatures into rarer forms and build resources while hiding behind the public identity of Dollar.
    - He grows from scavenger to dangerous hunter through Steel Armour Shelter conflicts, sacred-blood hunts, beast-soul armor, archery, martial training, and repeated clashes with Son of Heaven, Qin Xuan, and other shelter powers.
    - Han Sen discovers that sacred-blood genes are not the ceiling when he encounters super creatures and consumes the golden growler's life essence, learning that "super genes" exist beyond known human evolution.
    - By the end of the arc, Han Sen has turned Dollar into a legend, accumulated rare beast souls and geno points, and prepares to leave the first shelter as a far more dangerous evolver than anyone expected.

Show Han Sen as a young black-clad hunter in a stony alien creek valley, crouched over a metallic sacred-blood golden-black beetle while a starry black crystal glows in his hand. Add faint beast-soul armor energy and distant Steel Armour Shelter silhouettes. Reserve a clean top third of misty blue-gray sky and cliff shadow for the title, plus a quieter bottom band for author text. Style: polished fantasy sci-fi, dramatic but grounded, detailed subject, restrained background, no logos, no readable text, no gore.

## Book 2: Second God's Sanctuary
- Chapters 425-882
- Major plot points:
    - Han Sen and Zero arrive in the Second God's Sanctuary near a snowy spirit shelter, forcing Han Sen to rebuild from a dangerous new starting point with Zero as an unsettling but powerful companion.
    - Han Sen discovers the Crystal Palace Shelter, a mobile underwater crystal sailboat controlled by Mermaid Princess, and eventually defeats the golden crab guarding it, turning the ocean into his hunting ground.
    - The silver fox becomes one of Han Sen's most important companions, protecting him, healing him, and eventually tearing open the sky to ascend in a way that hints at stranger rules beyond normal evolution.
    - Han Sen's Dollar identity becomes a public human miracle after the brutal Divinity's Bout victory over Light Son of God, making him the first human Son of God in the Second Sanctuary.
    - Han Sen refines his Blood-Pulse Sutra, deep-sea hunts from Crystal Palace, accumulates super resources, and closes the arc preparing to become a surpasser and enter the Third God's Sanctuary.

## Book 3: Third God's Sanctuary
- Chapters 883-1338
- Major plot points:
    - Han Sen enters the Third God's Sanctuary under severe pressure, temporarily weakened by crystallized holy light while humanity begins learning how to refine Super Geno Essences.
    - He builds a human foothold by claiming shelters, gathering spirits and super creatures, and using allies such as Moment Queen, Little Silver, Zero, Golden Growler, and Xie Qing King.
    - Saint Fan Emperor returns as a major threat, bringing the Holy Rhino and an army tied to Thunder Hell Shelter, forcing Han Sen to defend his new territory and expose deeper emperor-shelter mechanics.
    - The Three-Life Tree, Lotus and Saint Fan threads, and Thunder Hell conflicts expand the sanctuary's spirit politics while Han Sen keeps pushing toward ten gene locks and demigod status.
    - Ruin Emperor and the strongest Third Sanctuary spirits target Han Sen and Zero, but Han Sen's raw physical power and Zero's Bone Dagger permanently destroy spirit stones, breaking the old assumption that emperors can always return.
    - Han Sen secures a protected human domain, finishes the Blood-Pulse Sutra's tenth lock, and prepares to step into the Fourth God's Sanctuary as a demigod.

## Book 4: Fourth and Fifth God's Sanctuaries
- Chapters 1339-1712
- Major plot points:
    - Han Sen becomes a demigod and enters the Fourth God's Sanctuary, where shelters revolve around Demigod Geno Cores and survival starts over under harsher rules.
    - He maxes out geno points and raises multiple geno cores, using the Crystal Core, Coin, and other powers to push toward super class while learning what lies beyond the known sanctuary system.
    - Dollar re-enters the Divinity's Bout and shocks the Fourth Sanctuary by defeating Six Paths, turning the Coin geno core into a symbol of overwhelming pressure and making Dollar famous across the universe.
    - Jade Shura's appearance in the Divinity's Bout deepens the mystery around Zero, the Shura Queen bloodline, God-slayer Luo, and the Falsified-Sky Sutra.
    - The black-armored man from the Iron Chimenea emerges as a terrifying unknown power, killing the Outer Sky leader and warning Han Sen that even his new strength has limits.

## Book 6: The Extreme King
- Chapters 2210-2467
- Major plot points:
    - Han Sen leaves Narrow Moon with Bao'er, Zero, Little Silver, and other companions as guards for Mister White, joining the Ice Blue Knights' expedition into the Extreme King's systems of chaos.
    - The systems of chaos reveal scale and danger far beyond normal space: Black Hole Spiders, Galactic Shrimp, and Under Overbearing show why even the Extreme King fear the region.
    - Han Sen operates under Ice Blue Knight authority while keeping his own team together, mapping xenogeneics, exploring ruins, and uncovering the Extreme King's brutality and internal secrets.
    - The Ice Blue arc escalates when Han Sen crosses the Extreme King, takes Bai Wuchang hostage, and repeatedly survives pursuit by powers that should be far above him.
    - Han Sen gains or exposes major treasures and mysteries, including the Shadow Queen Bee, deified relics, Medusa's Gaze, and connections back to Sacred, Crystallisers, and the Story of Genes.

## Book 7: The Very High and Outer Sky
- Chapters 2468-2719
- Major plot points:
    - Han Sen’s public legend grows after streamed battles where he defeats major King and half-deified elites, then uses the Blood-Pulse Sutra to repair and elevate Lando, making outsiders view him as someone who can “create” deified beings.
    - In the core area, Han Sen/Dollar keeps collecting absurdly high-level treasures and victories: the Dragon Tooth Dagger, Shield of Medusa’s Gaze, Charming God’s Jian, and kills or helps kill deified xenogeneics such as the Gold Armour General.
    - The Crocodile God arc reveals a ruined Crystalliser-style fortress inside a planet, tying Han Sen’s current universe back to older Crystalliser mysteries and gene-potential research.
    - The Very High enter the story heavily. Exquisite, Li Keer, and Bixi test Han Sen’s talent and pressure him into the “silkworm” system. Han Yan also enters Sky Palace and proves herself by walking the Sky Path and collecting holy gourds.
    - Han Sen explores Sky Palace’s stranger secrets, especially Black King City, where he finds a hidden human-like society and more evidence that humans have a deeper, stranger place in the universe than expected.
    - Han Sen finally agrees to go with Exquisite to the Very High’s Outer Sky, mostly because he needs resources and access to the Constellation Sea.
    - In Outer Sky, Han Sen becomes the center of the silkworm bouts. He defeats multiple elite silkworms, faces Shale after Shale returns to deified level, levels up to half-deified mid-fight, and develops his Under the Sky knife mind enough to defeat Shale. He wins first place and receives the Apollo Set.
    - After the bouts, Han Sen’s power spikes again through new tools and knowledge: he uses the Xuan Yellow Sutra to reverse a deified Red Blood Demon back down in level, gains deified geno cores, and refines an ice geno art with a powerful sleep/freezing effect.
    - The Four Sheep Cube and Underworld Lake introduce a major ominous thread. The cube is taboo among the Very High because past people who encountered it later betrayed them. Bao’er and Han Sen are pulled into the strange Sea Palace, a place with silent water and a clock-like sun.
    - The ending sets up larger god-level mysteries. Han Sen experiments with the black crystal on an Immortal Dragon, creating or revealing “geno protoplasm,” which draws the attention of Empty God’s true body. The black crystal armor hides Han Sen’s real power, implying the Geno Hall and god spirits are now directly closing in.
    - Overall, Book 7 shifts Han Sen from “powerful outsider in Sky Palace” to a universe-level anomaly. He becomes half-deified, wins the Very High silkworm contest, gains deified gear and skills, and starts brushing against secrets connected to the Geno Hall, god spirits, the black crystal, and the origin of humans.

Show Han Sen in the Very High's Outer Sky arena during the silkworm bouts, developing Under the Sky knife mind while facing Shale beneath a vast starry Constellation Sea. Include faint silhouettes of Exquisite and Li Keer, the Four Sheep Cube as an ominous relic, and a black crystal armor glimmer. Reserve a clean top third for title text and a subdued bottom band for author text. Style: elegant cosmic fantasy sci-fi, high-pressure tournament and mystery, no logos, no readable text.

    - Sky Vine Radish's godlike crisis mutates Space Garden, creates deified plant xenogeneics, wounds the universe, and causes the Geno Hall to appear in a way that reveals gods can be challenged.
    - Han Sen obtains a god spirit gene bone and begins testing whether god-spirit power can fight the gods who normally cannot be touched by ordinary beings.
    - Space Garden becomes Han Sen's independent power base, drawing Flower God allegiance, deified allies, and the attention of every faction that wants either its resources or Han Sen himself.
    - Han Sen's reputation explodes during the geno god list era as "Godfather Han," a figure believed capable of creating or elevating deified beings.
    - The Holy Kirin, sacred-beast remains, Sacred Leader's sister, and Xuan Yellow Sutra threads deepen the war-against-gods premise and point toward old Sacred-era secrets behind the universe's current order.

## Book 9: God Spirit Blood-Pulse
- Chapters 2970-3217
- Major plot points:
    - Han Sen is pulled into the universe of kingdoms, where power is organized around God Spirit Blood-Pulses, gene races, temples, and kingdom law instead of sanctuary-style geno progression.
    - His relationship with Qin Bai places him inside Qin Kingdom politics, where nobles, royals, teachers, and old promises all compete around the crown prince.
    - Han Sen's unusual power and hidden God of Wealth identity make others suspect he possesses an Annihilation-class God Spirit Blood-Pulse or another impossible blessing.
    - The Gold Crystal System reveals a super God Spirit Blood-Pulse and wild gene-race opportunities, drawing nobles and officers into a scramble for gene eggs and blood-pulse inheritance.
    - Jian Bu Gu, Thousand Mile Reach, Qin Jing Zhen, and other Qin figures expose how fragile the Qin Kingdom's political order is beneath its law system.
    - The arc turns toward Qin Xiu and Wan'er when the legendary alpha princess' body is found in ice, revealing a reverse Super God Spirit-like golden power tied to Qin bloodline myths.

## Book 10: The Thirty-Three Skies
- Chapters 3218-3463
- Major plot points:
    - The final arc centers on the Thirty-Three Skies, the geno tablet, and the unstable boundary between the geno universe and the universe of kingdoms.
    - Han Sen becomes trapped in spirit-body form near the Thirty-Third Sky and learns from Ancient Devil that the geno tablet is the foundation of the universe and can seal or control the broken skies.
    - Qin Xiu, the God Chaos Party, Ancient Devil, Big Sky Demon, Bury Path God, and other old powers converge around the geno tablet, each trying to shape the coming universal order.
    - Bao'er is trained as a possible answer to Qin Xiu, while Han Sen fights to gain a leader seat himself so she does not have to face the final battle alone.
    - The geno tablet fights gather the leaders of the skies, including Han Sen, Qin Xiu, Human King, Wang Yuhang, Ning Yue, Demon Alpha, and other familiar powers, turning the finale into a cosmic tournament over reality itself.
    - Han Sen ultimately refines the geno tablet's spirit through the sky clock, stops the collapse caused by reversed time and space, and preserves a changed universe at the cost of imperfectly restored lives.
    - The epilogue closes on Han Sen's family after the cosmic crisis, with Ji Yanran altered by the damaged cause chain and the surviving companions trying to live in the repaired world.
