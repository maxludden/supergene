For the Apple models, I used Apple’s official tech specs where available: iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 16e, and iPhone 15 all list width, height, depth, and weight directly.  

For Samsung, I used Samsung official pages where possible. Samsung lists the Galaxy A06 as **167.3 × 77.3 × 8.0 mm**, while Samsung’s Galaxy S25 Ultra press/spec pages list **162.8 × 77.6 × 8.2 mm** and **218 g**.
